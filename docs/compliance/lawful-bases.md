# Lawful Bases

Last updated: 2026-05-03

This document maps Profiley's main processing activities to the lawful basis relied upon in practice.

## Contract

Use contract where the processing is necessary to provide the product the user signed up for.

- Account creation and authentication
- Profile hosting and publication controls
- Document upload, extraction, chunking, and AI persona generation
- Core product emails and service notices

## Legitimate interest

Use legitimate interest where Profiley is operating, securing, or improving the service and the impact on the data subject is proportionate.

- Abuse prevention and rate limiting
- Moderation of prompts and generated content
- Reliability logging, provider diagnostics, and limited analytics
- Recruiter visit and interaction telemetry within the documented retention windows

## Consent

Use consent only for optional communications or features that are not required to provide the core service.

- Optional product communications and surveys
- Any future non-essential cookies or tracking not yet in production

## Legal obligation

Use legal obligation when Profiley must keep or disclose data under applicable law.

- Accounting / tax retention if applicable
- Responding to lawful regulator or court requests
- Some incident-response and records-preservation scenarios

## Important constraint

Recruiter-facing AI outputs are not to be framed as automated decision-making under Article 22 GDPR. They are assistive outputs only and must not be used as the sole basis for hiring or employment decisions.
