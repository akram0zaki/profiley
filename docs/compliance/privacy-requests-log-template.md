## Suggested status values

- `Open`
- `Awaiting verification`
- `In progress`
- `Completed`
- `Partially refused`
- `Refused`
- `Extended`

## Minimum note content

- Why the request was classified the way it was
- How identity was verified
- Which tables / files were included or excluded
- Whether any exception or legal hold applied
- Date and channel of the final response
