# Privacy Request Response Templates

Last updated: 2026-05-03

Use these templates as starting points. Adjust the scope and facts so they match the logged case exactly.

## Acknowledgement

"We received your privacy request and are verifying the scope. We aim to respond within one calendar month under the GDPR. If we need additional verification or more time, we will let you know."

## Verification request

"Before we continue, please confirm this request from the email address linked to your Profiley account or sign in and reply from the product flow. This helps us avoid disclosing personal data to the wrong person."

## Self-service export prompt

"For the standard scope of your Profiley account data, please sign in and use Settings -> Export your data. If you also need stored document files or a reviewed expansion beyond the standard export bundle, reply to this message and we will handle that manually after verification."

## Extension notice

"Your request is still being processed. Because of its scope or complexity, Profiley needs additional time. We expect to respond no later than two further calendar months from today."

## Completion notice

"Your privacy request has been completed. If you believe the response is incomplete, reply to this email. You also have the right to complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens."

## Partial refusal

"We completed your request in part, but we did not include some information because doing so would disclose another person's personal data or because a legal or safety exception applies. The excluded items are described below."

## Closure checklist

- Confirm the final response date in `privacy-requests-log-template.md`.
- Record any excluded tables, files, or legal holds.
- Record whether the requester used the self-service export, received manual follow-up, or both.
