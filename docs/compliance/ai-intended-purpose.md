# AI Intended Purpose

Last updated: 2026-05-03

Profiley's AI features are intended to help a user present their own professional background and help a recruiter or visitor navigate that information more efficiently.

## In-scope uses

- Generating recruiter-facing answers from the profile owner's uploaded materials and profile data
- Generating assistive job-fit analyses that compare a pasted role description against the profile owner's materials
- Helping the profile owner preview and improve the information exposed by their public profile
- Moderating abusive or unsafe inputs and enforcing prompt-safety rules

## Intended decision role

Profiley's AI is assistive only. It may support triage, review preparation, or question generation. It is not intended to make or recommend a final employment decision on its own.

## Intended operators

- The profile owner, who decides what data to upload, publish, or remove
- Recruiters or visitors, who may use the outputs as a starting point for human review
- The operator of Profiley, who manages providers, retention, moderation, and compliance
